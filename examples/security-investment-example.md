# Worked Example: Security & Resilience Investment

Security and resilience are useful examples because conventional ROI can encourage false precision.

## Scenario

An organisation proposes investment in:
- stronger endpoint/network detection;
- improved disaster recovery capability;
- identity controls;
- resilience testing.

## Poor business case

> A cyber incident could cost $5M, therefore spending $500K produces 900% ROI.

The calculation implies certainty that does not exist.

## Better case

Establish:
- material risks being treated;
- current control gaps;
- regulatory/contractual expectations;
- recovery objectives;
- incident history;
- current detection/recovery capability;
- cost of options;
- operational impact.

## Value measures

Possible measures:
- control coverage;
- mean time to detect/respond;
- recovery test success;
- systems meeting target RTO/RPO;
- critical vulnerabilities/exposures;
- audit/control findings;
- concentration risk reduced.

## Value statement

> The investment reduces material operational and cyber exposure, improves recovery confidence against agreed RTO/RPO targets, and strengthens evidence for customer and compliance requirements.

This is financially disciplined without pretending that risk reduction is guaranteed revenue.
