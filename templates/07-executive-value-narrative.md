# Executive Technology Value Narrative

## 1. Money
Where are we against budget and forecast? What materially changed?

## 2. Value
What measurable benefit has been realised?

## 3. Risk
What exposure increased or decreased?

## 4. Capability
What can the organisation now do better, faster or more safely?

## 5. Decision
What executive choice, funding decision or trade-off is required?

## One-paragraph summary
Write the story in business language before adding technology detail.
