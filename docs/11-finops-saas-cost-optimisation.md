# FinOps, SaaS & Cost Optimisation

Cost optimisation should be continuous, not an emergency exercise triggered by a budget problem.

## Common optimisation areas

### SaaS
- inactive users;
- premium licences without premium-feature use;
- duplicate tools;
- overlapping departmental subscriptions;
- unmanaged renewals;
- shelfware.

### Cloud
- idle compute;
- over-provisioning;
- storage lifecycle;
- data-egress patterns;
- reserved/committed-use suitability;
- test/dev shutdown;
- architecture choices affecting consumption.

### Services and vendors
- overlapping scopes;
- unused retainers;
- excessive fragmentation;
- weak service levels;
- support tiers not aligned to criticality.

## Optimisation guardrail

Do not optimise cost in a way that destroys value.

Examples:
- reducing resilience to save infrastructure cost;
- cutting support coverage below business need;
- removing licences that users genuinely need;
- forcing consolidation into a platform that cannot support the capability.

The objective is **economic efficiency**, not simply lower spend.
